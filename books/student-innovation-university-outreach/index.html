About the Book

Universities are increasingly recognised as engines of social, economic and environmental change beyond their campuses. Through student-led innovation, service learning, living labs, incubators and community partnerships, higher education institutions can co-create practical solutions to local sustainability challenges and help advance the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

This book brings together research, frameworks and case studies that examine how students, faculty and communities collaborate to design, test and scale innovations for sustainable communities. It aims to serve as a reference for academicians, researchers, students, university leaders, policymakers, NGOs and industry partners interested in engaged scholarship and community-driven sustainability.

Objective

To showcase models and evidence of how student innovation and university outreach strengthen sustainable communities.

Target Audience

Researchers, faculty, students, higher-education administrators, policymakers, NGOs and industry practitioners.

Contribution Types

Original research, conceptual frameworks, reviews, case studies and best-practice reports.
